# My Favorite Series and Movies

## Haikyuu!!
*Haikyuu!!* is an inspiring sports anime that follows Shoyo Hinata, a passionate volleyball player determined to prove that height doesn’t define skill. Together with his teammates, he learns about teamwork, perseverance, and the spirit of competition as they aim to reach the national championships.


## Money Heist
*Money Heist* (La Casa de Papel) is a thrilling Spanish series about a group of robbers who plan and execute intricate heists under the guidance of their mysterious leader, the Professor. The story combines strategy, emotion, and rebellion as the team fights for their goals while facing intense moral and personal struggles.


## Kakegurui
*Kakegurui* is a psychological anime set in Hyakkaou Private Academy, where students determine their social rank through gambling. The series follows Yumeko Jabami, a fearless transfer student who gambles for the thrill rather than the win, revealing the dark and unpredictable side of human nature.


## Reply 1998
*Reply 1998* is a heartwarming Korean drama that portrays the everyday lives of friends and families in a small neighborhood during the late 1990s. It captures the beauty of nostalgia, friendship, and growing up, making it both touching and relatable.


## Ratatouille
*Ratatouille* is an animated film about Remy, a rat who dreams of becoming a chef in Paris. Despite the odds, he proves that anyone can achieve greatness with talent, passion, and determination. The movie celebrates creativity and following one’s dreams.


## Whiplash
*Whiplash* tells the intense story of Andrew Neiman, a young drummer striving for greatness under the strict guidance of his demanding music instructor, Terence Fletcher. It’s a powerful portrayal of ambition, sacrifice, and the pursuit of perfection in the world of music.


## My Best Friend’s Wedding
*My Best Friend’s Wedding* is a romantic comedy about a woman who realizes she’s in love with her best friend—just as he’s about to get married. It explores love, timing, and acceptance, showing that sometimes letting go is the most genuine form of love.
